## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-impact digital craftsmanship built specifically for performance-driven landing pages and conversion infrastructure. The visual direction fuses a sophisticated dark aesthetic with vibrant, high-energy emerald and mint accents, evoking immediate psychological cues of financial return, revenue acceleration, and operational mastery.

### Core Character
- **Target Audience:** Modern Indonesian business owners, D2C founders, high-growth startup leaders, and marketing executives seeking tier-one landing pages with aggressive ROI.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep, calculated, and elite. It avoids noisy decorative clutter in favor of surgical precision: crisp borders, disciplined grid alignments, and hyper-legible metric displays.
- **Design Movement:** Modern Technical Dark Mode with subtle ambient glows, tactical micro-surfaces, and crisp functional typography.

### Design Principles
1. **Conversion Velocity:** Every CTA, badge, and interaction point carries deliberate visual weight, guiding the eye directly toward conversion triggers like direct WhatsApp routing and high-intent inquiry forms.
2. **Proof & Metrics Over Hype:** Social proof, ROI counters (+140% Conversion, 3.4x ROAS), and trust seals are treated as foundational structural components rather than decorative afterthoughts.
3. **Engineered Precision:** Tight micro-borders, controlled luminosity layers, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy communicate unmatched execution quality.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around a nocturnal spectrum anchored by deep obsidian and slate foundations, punctuated by vibrant conversion emeralds and performance mint tones.

### Color Roles & Implementation
- **Primary Emerald (`#10B981`):** Reserved for primary triggers, conversion metric indicators, focal buttons, and active interactive states. Signifies growth and transaction momentum.
- **Secondary Deep Emerald (`#059669`):** Deployed for active state gradients, badge borders, and foundational accents that provide depth beneath primary bright points.
- **Tertiary Mint Highlight (`#34D399`):** Used strictly for high-visibility highlights, glowing drop shadows, and micro-metric emphasis tags.
- **Neutral Dark Base (`#090D12`):** The primary canvas background. Layered with slightly lighter surface tiers (`#0F151D`, `#161F2B`, and `#1E293B`) to establish distinct visual containment without harsh transitions.
- **WhatsApp Accent (`#25D366`):** Harmonizes with the emerald palette while maintaining authentic brand familiarity for Indonesian conversion paths.

### Palette Hierarchy Rules
1. Background surfaces must remain deep and unsaturated to ensure green luminescence cuts through with immediate focus.
2. Text should use high-clarity neutrals: `#F8FAFC` for headlines, `#94A3B8` for supportive body copy, and `#64748B` for subdued metadata.
3. Never use vibrant green across large structural backgrounds; restrict it to actionable buttons, data callouts, and delicate ambient glow gradients.

## Typography

The typographic stack balances progressive technical authority with high-speed readability. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a structural, high-conviction cadence suited for bold sales propositions, punchy client claims, and conversion headlines. Its geometric construction feels engineered and modern.
- **Body & Editorial (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a warm, clean, and highly readable canvas for explanatory copy, service breakdowns, and testimonials, maintaining legibility even at small scales on mobile screens.
- **Metrics & Technical Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied exclusively to performance proof metrics (+140%, 3.8x ROI, 0.4s Load Speed), status chips, and tabular data points to reinforce analytical rigor and technical precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column responsive fluid grid system designed to support aggressive scannability and modular section stacking.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of 1240px, `gutter` of 1.5rem, and outer `margin` of 2rem to ensure content never collides with viewport borders.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 1.25rem gutters. Pricing tables and feature cards condense into paired matrices.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with 1rem margins and gutters. All metric grids and process sequences collapse vertically into singular, high-converting scroll stacks.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence follows an exponential 8pt rhythm. Sections are spaced broadly using `space-xl` and extended vertical gaps (80px–120px) to give critical conversion hooks clear breathing room. Component internal padding relies strictly on `space-sm` for compact indicators, `space-md` for standard forms and cards, and `space-lg` for prominent pricing tiers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids traditional drop shadows, favoring dark tactical illumination, low-contrast ghost borders, and focused ambient glows.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- **Canvas (Level 0):** `#090D12` base darkness.
- **Surface Cards (Level 1):** `#0F151D` layered with a crisp border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Raised Interactive Surfaces (Level 2):** `#161F2B` layered with active emerald rim lights: `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts (Level 3):** `#1A2433` with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) and subtle outer perimeter shadow (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`).

### Emerald Glow & Atmospheric Light
Key conversion modules (such as the featured pricing package or sticky CTA container) employ an ambient emerald aura:
- **Subtle Glow:** `box-shadow: 0 0 35px -8px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.
- **Hero Interactive Hover:** `box-shadow: 0 0 25px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.
- **Glow Divider:** Soft gradient borders running horizontally with `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4), transparent)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined shape profile (`roundedness: 2`), applying 0.5rem (8px) corners to core containers, with larger modules scaling up to 1rem (16px) and 1.5rem (24px).

### Curvature Taxonomy
- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** 0.5rem (8px) for a modern, engineered feel that avoids childlike bubble aesthetics while eliminating harsh industrial points.
- **Content Cards & Testimonial Containers:** 1rem (16px) outer boundary, ensuring structured layout division across dense content.
- **Hero Feature Shells & Media Wrappers:** 1.5rem (24px) for expansive framing.
- **Metric Micro-Chips:** Pill-style radiuses used exclusively on quantitative badges (`rounded-full`) to create a clear visual distinction between static content and key performance metrics.

## Components

### Buttons & Conversion Triggers
- **Primary Conversion Button (WhatsApp / Direct Action):** Heavy green fill (`#10B981`) paired with dark contrasting text (`#090D12`), weight 600. Includes an integrated forward chevron or WhatsApp icon. On hover, applies an emerald bloom effect (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`) and slight upward lift (-1px).
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Translucent dark fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), crisp border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), white text (`#F8FAFC`). Hover state brightens border to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.5)`.
- **Sticky Floating Mobile Action Bar:** Fixed to the bottom viewport with safe-area spacing, housing a direct WhatsApp trigger button with an unmissable pulse indicator.

### Micro-Metrics & Performance Badges
- **Structure:** Pill-shaped badge featuring JetBrains Mono micro-text (`label-sm` or `metric-badge`).
- **Styling:** Subtle green surface (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), vibrant green border (`1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`), and high-contrast mint text (`#34D399`). Often paired with an up-trending micro-arrow (↑) or green dot indicator.

### Cards (Proof, Features & Case Studies)
- **Base Style:** Dark container (`#0F151D`), delicate outline (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), and 1rem padding.
- **Metric Highlights:** Case study cards feature top-pinned numeric badges (e.g., `+182% Conversion Rate`) placed before client copy.

### Pricing Packages
- **Standard Tier:** Neutral container (`#0F151D`), standard border, subdued action button.
- **Featured / Recommended Tier:** Bordered with radiant emerald accenting (`1px solid #10B981`), ambient green back-glow, prominent "Paling Dipilih" (Most Popular) pill tag on top edge, and solid emerald primary CTA.

### Input Fields & Inquiry Forms
- **Field Canvas:** Deep recessed fill (`#090D12`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` border, soft roundedness (0.5rem).
- **Focus State:** Transitions to `1px solid #10B981` with an ambient glow outline (`0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`). Placeholder copy remains clean and legible at `#64748B`.

### Interactive FAQ Accordion
- **Surface:** Seamless dark-slate panel with horizontal border dividers.
- **Interaction:** Smooth expansion revealing clear body text; the toggle indicator transforms from a clean plus (+) to a minus (−) with emerald color shifts upon activation.
